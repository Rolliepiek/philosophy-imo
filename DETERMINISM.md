I personally believe in determinism. That means I believe that everything is an outcome of a cause. Not only things such as a stone rolling down a hill, but also the human mind. I believe the deterministic cause isn't decided by some higher power such as god, instead, I think this cause is also an outcome of an earlier cause. I think chemicals and electricity in our brain is what makes us think what we think and do the things that we do. This kind of thinking is called radical causal determinism or hard determinism.
